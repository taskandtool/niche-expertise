# Social post

A platform shows a post to more people when its first viewers send it, save
it, comment or keep watching. Open with a moment, a number or a question the
reader recognises. Give them something worth keeping, and ask for one small
thing the platform rewards.

Example, for Instagram:

```
Three of the five boilers we saw this week had the same problem: pressure under 1 bar.

Check yours now. The gauge is on the front, usually bottom left. Between 1 and 1.5 is right when the heating's off. Under 1, top it up with the filling loop (there's a how-to video in our highlights).

Save this for the first cold morning.
```

## Steps

Copy and tick:

- [ ] 1. **Pillar and platform.** Pick the pillar: education, proof,
      behind the scenes, offer (at most one post in five) or community
      (`references/repurpose.md`). Read `references/<platform>.md` for the
      platform the post is for.
- [ ] 2. **The idea**, from the sources below.
- [ ] 3. **The hook and the words.** Make the first line stand alone. Put
      the hook and its payoff before the platform's cutoff (given in its
      `references/` file).
- [ ] 4. **The ask**, one, matched to what the platform rewards: "Save
      this", "Send this to someone planning a kitchen", "Reply with yours".
      Keep links out of the body on Facebook, LinkedIn and X.
- [ ] 5. **The picture**: the owner's photo as it is or edited, a
      carousel of real job photos, a slideshow (`references/slideshow.md`),
      a short clip, or one made from scratch (the Pictures section of
      `AGENTS.md`).
- [ ] 6. **File it for the owner** where and how this app's `AGENTS.md`
      says, with the whole caption as it will be pasted and any hashtags as
      one line (`"#sarasotacoffee #downtownsarasota"`). A week of posts is
      one review.
- [ ] 7. **Posting.** Once the owner approves, post it through their
      posting connection (Zernio, Hootsuite) at the time they chose. Then
      set `status: posted` and the live `url`. If they have no posting
      connection, the owner posts it.

## Where post ideas come from

Every enquiry and FAQ ("people always ask"); the job in hand (before and
after, a detail, one decision explained); reviews (the quote, then the story
behind it); the objection competitors leave unanswered; the season and the
local calendar; comments on the owner's own posts (answer them as posts);
the owner's own numbers and opinions; and any ad that won, recut for the
feed.

## One idea, a week of posts

Write the set as `references/repurpose.md` says. Rewrite the post for each
platform instead of pasting it across: Instagram and Facebook show less of
accounts that mostly repost or cross-post unchanged.

## Rules

- Suggest the owner replies to comments in the first hour.
- No engagement bait ("tag a friend"), no hashtag walls, no watermarked
  reposts.
- A review is quoted as written, with permission; customer photos with
  permission and credit.

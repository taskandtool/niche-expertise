# Where the seo skill comes from

Read in November 2026. Re-check Google's pages when a rule here is in doubt;
the studies are snapshots.

## Google

- AI features and your website: no extra requirements for AI Overviews or
  AI Mode; indexed with a snippet; no AI text files or special schema.
  https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features
- Spam policies, scaled content abuse: many pages made to rank, not to
  help, however they are made; includes stitching pages together and
  running several sites to hide scale.
  https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies
- Reviews system and writing high-quality reviews: ranked lists are judged
  on evidence, measurements, what sets each apart, drawbacks, best-for, and
  standing alone.
  https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/reviews-system
  https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/ecommerce/write-high-quality-reviews
- Review snippets: "Don't aggregate reviews or ratings from other
  websites"; no rating on a list of items.
  https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/review-snippet
- Carousel (ItemList): rich result only with courses, movies, recipes and
  restaurants.
  https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/carousel
- FAQ rich results: only for well-known government and health sites
  (changelog, 2023).

## Studies

- Aggarwal et al., GEO: Generative Engine Optimization (KDD 2024,
  arXiv:2311.09735): statistics, quotations and cited sources raised
  visibility in generative answers by up to about 40%, while keyword
  stuffing lowered it.
- Spearleaf, How ChatGPT picks a local business (August 2026, 216 US
  markets): an in-city address was the trait most associated with being
  shown; businesses that stayed had more reviews at the same rating;
  best-of list sites among the most cited.
  https://spearleaf.com/research/how-chatgpt-picks-a-local-business/
- Peekl, Which websites AI trusts for local recommendations (October 2026,
  1,926 Gemini answers): guides and best-of lists 11% of sources; Whitespark's
  2026 survey put expert best-of lists as the top factor for AI visibility.
  https://peekl.io/ai-visibility/guides/which-websites-does-ai-trust
- Ahrefs (2026, as reported; the post itself not read): about 38% of AI Overview citations rank in the organic top
  10 for the same query, down from 76% in 2025. Vendor studies of tables and
  freshness point the same way but publish little method.
